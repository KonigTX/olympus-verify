# High Council portfolios

Twelve reserved Council portfolios serve under the Guild Master as chair. Each portfolio has a distinct remit; all share the High Council native rank.

## Guild Master — Council Chair

The alliance Guild Master chairs common Council business while retaining the separate Leader classification. Chairing does not add another native rank or a second vote.

## Hand of the Sovereign / Co-Guild Master

Alliance executive coordination, continuity and cross-branch liaison within the accepted remit. Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are not added to this remit by implication.

## Lord High Treasurer

Alliance budgets, receipts and financial stewardship, with custody, spending approval and independent audit kept separate.

## Lord High Chancellor / Head of Justice

The Lord High Chancellor is the High Council’s individual judicial portfolio holder. The Council of Justice and Privy Court remain institutions with separately designated hearing panels; holding this office is not a permanent seat on every case or a power to judge one’s own enforcement decision. Panel service gives no blanket Council vote, case access or enforcement power; each participant still needs case designation, relevant access and conflict review.

## Grand Marshal of PvE

The Grand Marshal of PvE directs alliance-wide raid preparation. This is the main raid portfolio on the High Council, distinct from the NA and EU Raid Leaders who command their own scheduled teams.

## Grand Marshal of PvP

The Grand Marshal of PvP coordinates voluntary battleground teams and world-PvP activity across Olympus. The office supplies strategy and reliable event leadership within a PvE guild; participation in PvP is never a condition of ordinary membership.

## High Inquisitor / Minister of Internal Security

The High Inquisitor leads the preventive safety and conduct programme. The office helps the separately authorized moderation team detect abuse and preserve proportionate evidence; it does not decide its own contested appeals or create a right to inspect private messages.

## High Minister of the Chancery & Imperial Census

Alliance admission standards, intake coordination and escalation, distinct from branch Recruiters and their local capacity decisions.

## Lord High Envoy / Director of the Concordat

The Grand Concordat Director coordinates the ten sister branches while preserving each Guild Master’s local command. The office turns dated capacity evidence, branch contacts and member preferences into fair routing proposals; it cannot manufacture seats or issue automatic invitations.

## Grand Master Artisan

Alliance profession policy, voluntary craft coordination and Workshop stewardship, with local suppliers and emissaries keeping their own remits.

## Minister of Propaganda / Chief Propagandist

Leads recruitment campaigns, guild storytelling, promotional art and broadcaster-safe messaging. Agrees creative direction with the Council, verifies claims with the responsible office and hands official records to Heraldry. The office grants no moderation, Council vote or publishing access by itself.

## Lord High Herald / Minister of Heraldry

Alliance identity, approved communications and publication standards, coordinated through responsible local editors and emissaries.

## Lord High Merchant / Head of Trading & Markets

A reserved High Council portfolio devoted solely to Auction House optimization, direct buy/sell negotiation and the market economy. The Treasurer authorizes capital and risk limits and retains custody; realized results and inventory are independently reviewable. No holder or access is granted by this reference.

Architecture and engineering remain technical support appointments outside the High Council. Regional Raid Leaders and PvP Leads retain their separate Officer Corps responsibilities. The Treasurer controls authorized custody and budgets; Trading manages market execution; Audit and Justice remain independent of the work under review.

Council appointments, voting eligibility and actual access are separately recorded. Listing a portfolio appoints no holder.
