# The Imperial Cabinet of Olympus

The Guild Master chairs the High Council. The Co-Guild Master serves alongside twelve numbered portfolios. Accepted warrants and separate grants establish individual appointments and access.

## Hand of the Sovereign · Co-Guild Master

Stable office: `office_19`. Alliance executive coordination, continuity and cross-branch liaison within the accepted remit. Vice Guild Master and Chief of Staff are not added to this remit by implication.

## 1. Lord High Treasurer · Minister of the Imperial Exchequer

Stable office: `office_86`. Alliance budgets, receipts and financial stewardship, with custody, spending approval and independent audit kept separate.

## 2. Lord High Justiciar · Minister of Justice & The Privy Court

Stable office: `office_101`. The Lord High Justiciar is the High Council’s individual judicial portfolio holder. The Council of Justice and Privy Court remain institutions with separately designated hearing panels; holding this office is not a permanent seat on every case or a power to judge one’s own enforcement decision. Panel service gives no blanket Council vote, case access or enforcement power; each participant still needs case designation, relevant access and conflict review.

## 3. Lord High Marshal of PvE · Minister of Raid Campaigns

Stable office: `office_97`. The Lord High Marshal of PvE directs alliance-wide raid preparation. This is the main raid portfolio on the High Council, distinct from the NA and EU Raid Leaders who command their own scheduled teams.

## 4. Lord High Marshal of PvP · Minister of Battlegrounds & Honor

Stable office: `office_98`. The Lord High Marshal of PvP coordinates voluntary battleground teams and world-PvP activity across Olympus. The office supplies strategy and reliable event leadership within a PvE guild; participation in PvP is never a condition of ordinary membership.

## 5. Lord High Inquisitor · Minister of Internal Security

Stable office: `office_99`. The Lord High Inquisitor leads the preventive safety and conduct programme. The office helps the separately authorized moderation team detect abuse and preserve proportionate evidence; it does not decide its own contested appeals or create a right to inspect private messages.

## 6. Lord High Chancellor · Minister of the Chancery & Imperial Census

Stable office: `office_14`. Alliance admission standards, intake coordination and escalation, distinct from branch Recruiters and their local capacity decisions.

## 7. Lord High Legate · Minister of the Grand Concordat

Stable office: `office_100`. The Lord High Legate coordinates the ten sister branches while preserving each Guild Master’s local command. The office turns dated capacity evidence, branch contacts and member preferences into fair routing proposals; it cannot manufacture seats or issue automatic invitations.

## 8. Lord High Artificer · Minister of Artisanry & The Guild Workshop

Stable office: `office_58`. Alliance profession policy, voluntary craft coordination and Workshop stewardship, with local suppliers and emissaries keeping their own remits.

## 9. Lord High Herald · Minister of Propaganda

Stable office: `office_102`. Leads official communications, recruitment campaigns, guild storytelling, promotional art and consenting broadcaster coverage. Verifies claims with the responsible office and maintains approved publication and correction records. Coordinates event and Academy communications with their responsible teams. The office grants no moderation, Council vote or publishing access by itself.

## 10. Lord High Seneschal · Minister of Court Protocol & Crown Estates

Stable office: `office_59`. Court protocol, ceremonial precedence, the court calendar and Crown facilities stewardship. Ownership, treasury custody, publicity and judicial decisions remain separately assigned.

## 11. Lord High Factor · Minister of Trading & Markets

Stable office: `office_96`. A reserved High Council portfolio devoted solely to Auction House optimization, direct buy/sell negotiation and the market economy. The Treasurer authorizes capital and risk limits and retains custody; realized results and inventory are independently reviewable. No holder or access is granted by this reference.

## 12. Systems & Heraldry

Stable office: `office_103`. Sets technical stewardship, recovery and release evidence standards, and maintains the guild’s standards for arms, insignia, titles and attribution. Systems operators keep their own access grants; the Herald leads campaigns and the Seneschal directs court protocol.
